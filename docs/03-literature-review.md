## 3.2. Synthesis and Architectural Conclusion
The literature review confirms two critical domain findings:
1. **Why Pure Keyword Search Fails:** Sparse lexical models (SQL `ILIKE`, TF-IDF, BM25) cannot bridge the vocabulary gap between colloquial citizen queries and statutory terminology, especially in morphologically rich languages like Kazakh and Russian.
2. **Why Unconstrained Generative Chatbots Are Unsafe for GovTech:** Generative LLMs frequently fabricate non-existent laws, outdated fees, or incorrect document lists. Therefore, our architecture implements **deterministic hybrid retrieval**: NLP is used strictly to understand the user's intent and rank verified `PostgreSQL` service passports.
